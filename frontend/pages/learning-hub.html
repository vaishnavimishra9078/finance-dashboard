01

Stock Market Basics

A stock represents partial ownership in a company. When you buy a share, you own a small piece of that business and can benefit if it grows in value or pays dividends.

Stocks trade on exchanges like the NYSE or Nasdaq. Prices move based on supply and demand — driven by company performance, economic news, and investor sentiment.

Key terms

Bull Market Prices generally rising
Bear Market Prices generally falling
Dividend Profit paid to shareholders
Market Cap Company's total market value
02

Key Metrics Explained

P/E Ratio

Shows how much investors pay for each dollar of company earnings.

EPS

Earnings per share measures the profit attributable to each share.

Market Cap

The total market value of a company's outstanding shares.

Beta

Measures a stock's volatility relative to the overall market.

03

Diversification

Diversification means spreading investments across different assets, sectors and geographies.

If one stock or sector performs poorly, other investments may help reduce the overall impact on your portfolio.

FINANCE PRINCIPLE Don't put all your eggs in one basket.
